Followers Exporter for LinkedIn Pages

Exports the latest followers of a LinkedIn Page you manage. Without a webhook, the button on LinkedIn downloads a CSV. With one, it sends the followers to your webhook in a single request.

Any endpoint that accepts a JSON POST works: an n8n Webhook node, a Make or Zapier webhook, a Clay webhook source... When you save, Chrome asks you to allow the extension to reach this one address. Leave empty to download CSV files.

How to use it

  1. Open your LinkedIn Page as an admin, then Analytics > Followers.
  2. Next to All followers, pick how many recent followers to read (10 to 5,000).
  3. Click the button. Keep the tab open and in front while it loads: Chrome slows down background tabs.

What your webhook receives

One POST with a JSON array, newest follower first:

[
  {
    "profile_url": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/someone/",
    "following_date": "2026-10",
    "name": "Someone",
    "headline": "Security Engineer at Example"
  }
]

following_date is a month (YYYY-MM): LinkedIn does not show the exact day.

Optional: show counts on LinkedIn

If your webhook answers with JSON such as {"new": 37, "known": 163}, for example after checking which followers you already have, those counts appear next to the button.
